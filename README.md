# Ranevo – Digital Classroom & Student Growth Platform

## Overview

Ranevo is a digital classroom and student growth platform designed to streamline educational operations and support personalized learning.

The platform centralizes student management, attendance tracking, assessments, fee management, learning resources, communication workflows, and AI-powered speaking feedback in a single system.

Inspired by real-world operational challenges observed in a spoken English and personality development institute, Ranevo aims to improve both administrative efficiency and student learning outcomes.

---

## Problem Statement

Educational institutes often rely on multiple disconnected tools such as spreadsheets, messaging applications, and cloud storage platforms to manage:

* Student records
* Attendance
* Assessments
* Fee tracking
* Learning materials
* Communication
* Progress monitoring

This leads to fragmented workflows, manual effort, and limited visibility into student growth.

---

## Solution

Ranevo provides a unified platform that enables administrators and students to manage and access essential academic and operational information through a centralized system.

The platform focuses on both operational management and student development.
